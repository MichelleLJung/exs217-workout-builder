EXS217 Cardio Workout Builder — Prototype 3

Purpose: Help students organize the main workout they design without making programming decisions for them.

Student flow:
1. Choose Exercises
2. Build Your Workout
3. Review Your Design

Warm-up and cool-down are intentionally not built in the tool. Review prompts remind students to account for them, demonstrations, transitions, intensity, modifications, equipment, and group management.
